What is this group?

  • A space for reading and discussing systems
  • Not a Rust fan club.
  • Hands-on learning space
  • Flat structure, everyone contributes to learning
📢Everyone can join, regardless of experience or number of classes containing the numbers 6 and 4 on your transcript or number of portraits of Linus Torvalds in your dorm room.

Bring a laptop, read or browse to the suggested material before. We will be reading during the sessions and hosting discussions afterwards. Everyone is expected to contribute with anything ranging from enlightening knowledge to questions and snacks.

You do not need to have attended past weeks.
