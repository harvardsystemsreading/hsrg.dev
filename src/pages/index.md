# Harvard Systems Reading Group

The Harvard Systems Reading Group is a collection of undergraduates at Harvard interested in all things systems, including but not limited to:
- Systems security
- Distributed systems
- ML systems
- Compilers
- Operating systems
- Infrastructure
- Hardware
- And more

Hosted by Heorhii Ambartsumov, Ethan Carter Edwards, Sam Huang, and Emmanuel Rassou. Everyone is welcome, regardless of experience.

<p class="muted"><em>Inspired by the Harvard Systems Reading Group hosted by <a href="https://www.ekzhang.com/">Eric Zhang</a>.</em></p>

### Meetings

**Saturdays @ 3PM**, Lowell underground lounge — from the entrance, turn right, then downstairs.

Join the [WhatsApp group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/BlxUGT0XxdV2uJqQNtEywO) for announcements.

Bring a laptop, and read or browse the suggested material beforehand. We read or present during the sessions and host discussions afterwards. You do not need to have attended past weeks.
