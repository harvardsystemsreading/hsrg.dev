# Harvard Systems Reading Group

Hosted by Heorhii Ambartsumov, Ethan Carter Edwards, Sam Huang, and Emmanuel Rassou. Everyone is welcome, regardless of experience.

<p class="muted"><em>Inspired by the Harvard Systems Reading Group hosted by <a href="https://www.ekzhang.com/">Eric Zhang</a>.</em></p>

### Meetings

**TBD**, Lowell underground lounge — from the entrance, turn right, then downstairs.

Join the [WhatsApp group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/BlxUGT0XxdV2uJqQNtEywO) for announcements.

Bring a laptop, and read or browse the suggested material beforehand. We read or present during the sessions and host discussions afterwards. Everyone is expected to contribute with anything ranging from enlightening knowledge to questions and snacks. You do not need to have attended past weeks.
